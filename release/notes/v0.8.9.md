# 5tratumOS 0.8.9 — licence backup and restore

Open **Settings → 5tratMux Licence → Back up or restore licence** in Classic
or Orbit. Back up your proof outside the PC, then upload it after a reinstall
or on a replacement PC. Paid licences support up to five self-service moves,
preserving the original plan and expiry. Keep the proof private: it contains
the recovery secret.

Each move retires the old installation's paid binding. The old PC stops at its
next online licence check or when its existing offline access expires; it
cannot be disabled instantly while disconnected.

This update installs the separately signed **5tratMux 0.9.52** through the
OS-owned updater and retains the 0.8.8 update handoff fix. Existing feature
packaging and settings are preserved. Fresh-install media remains at 0.8.7;
use Settings → Updates after installation.

The OS archive uses the established SHA-256 checksum mechanism. The native MUX
release manifest is independently signed; this does not sign the OS archive.
