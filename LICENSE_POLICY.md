## Public vs private repos

Public repo(s) are for:
- Transparency (auditable code, docs).
- Release artifacts (update bundles, hashes, signatures).

Private repo(s) may contain:
- Build pipelines and release automation.
- Bundled/minified portal assets and orchestration logic.
- Store curation logic and proprietary configuration.
