# 5tratumOS Trademark & Branding Policy

`5tratumOS`, the `5tratumOS` name, and the associated logos/marks are trademarks of the 5tratumOS project.

This document explains what you may and may not do with our branding.

## Allowed
- Referencing 5tratumOS by name when describing compatibility (e.g. "works with 5tratumOS").
- Linking to the official repositories and release pages.
- Using unmodified logos/marks in articles/reviews that are clearly about the official project.

## Not Allowed (without explicit written permission)
- Distributing modified builds using the `5tratumOS` name, logo, or confusingly similar branding.
- Selling, bundling, or pre-installing the software while representing it as official 5tratumOS.
- Using 5tratumOS branding in a way that implies endorsement, partnership, or certification.
- Registering domains, social handles, or product names that are confusingly similar to `5tratumOS`.

## Forks
Forks are welcome, but must:
- Use a different project name and different logos.
- Remove/replace 5tratumOS branding from UI, documentation, and assets.

## Attribution
If your fork is based on this project, clearly state that it is an independent fork and not affiliated with the official 5tratumOS project.

## Contact
For branding permissions, contact the maintainer(s) via the official repository issues or an email address listed there.
