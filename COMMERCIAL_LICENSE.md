# Commercial Licensing (System Builders / Resellers / Integrators)

If you want to build, provision, or sell devices/"nodes" for other people or businesses that include **5tratumOS** (including flashing/writing the installer or OS image), you need a **separate commercial license** from the Licensor.

This includes (examples):
- Selling, renting, leasing, or otherwise distributing hardware with 5tratumOS pre-installed.
- Shipping pre-flashed media (USB sticks, SSD/HDDs, SD cards) that contain 5tratumOS.
- Providing paid installation/provisioning services that deliver a 5tratumOS device/image to a customer.
- Offering 5tratumOS (or a derivative) as a hosted/managed service or competing product.

## What a commercial agreement can provide

A commercial license agreement can grant (as explicitly agreed in writing):
- Permission to distribute devices/images with 5tratumOS pre-installed.
- Permission to use official **5tratum/5tratumOS** branding and "official" tagging/marketing, subject to `TRADEMARK.md`.
- Optional commercial support/update terms (if offered).

## How to request a commercial license

- Open a GitHub issue in this repo describing what you want to do (countries, quantities, and use-case).
- If there is an official email/contact listed in the repo, you may contact us there instead.
