# Blockchain data / content disclaimer

Some blockchains can contain **arbitrary third‑party data** (including text, images, links, and other payloads). When you run a full node, you may **download, store, and relay** that data as part of normal network operation.

Depending on your jurisdiction, some blockchain content may be **illegal, harmful, or offensive**. 5tratumOS does not control the content embedded in blockchain data.

You are responsible for understanding the legal/compliance implications of running node software where you live and for choosing an appropriate configuration (for example pruning vs archival, and whether you serve peers).

**BSV note:** the Bitcoin SV (BSV) ecosystem has historically encouraged large on‑chain data payloads, which can increase both the *volume* and the *likelihood* of arbitrary content being stored or relayed.
