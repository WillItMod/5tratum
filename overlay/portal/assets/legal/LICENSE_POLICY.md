# Licensing Policy (Open‑Core)

This project is intended to be transparent, but not fork‑friendly for
redistribution/rebranding.

## What you can do (default)
- Run 5tratumOS on your own hardware.
- Modify it for your own internal use.
- Evaluate it locally.

## What you cannot do without permission
- Rebrand and redistribute it as your own OS/appliance.
- Ship images/appliances/bundles that include 5tratumOS (modified or not).
- Offer it as a hosted/managed service or use it to provide a competing product.

See `LICENSE` for the legally binding terms and `TRADEMARK.md` for branding.

## Public vs private repos

Public repo(s) are for:
- Transparency (auditable code, docs).
- Release artifacts (update bundles, hashes, signatures).

Private repo(s) may contain:
- Build pipelines and release automation.
- Bundled/minified portal assets and orchestration logic.
- Store curation logic and proprietary configuration.
